# Support

Need help with **Flappy Bird Reinvented**? Here is how to get support for gameplay issues, player accounts, or contributing to the project.

## Where to Get Help

* **Gameplay Bugs & Glitches**: If you run into collision issues, audio bugs, or UI glitches while playing, open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com) with details on what happened and what browser/device you were using.
* **Account, Cloud Save & Leaderboard Help**: Having trouble signing up, logging in, syncing unlocked skins/coins, or seeing your score on the Top 10 leaderboard? Email **greenhenry441@gmail.com** with your in-game username and a description of the issue.
* **Security Vulnerabilities & Exploits**: Do **not** post exploits or security issues publicly. Follow the instructions in our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and email **greenhenry441@gmail.com** directly.
* **Applying to Contribute**: Want to help build new features, power-ups, or art? Check out [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for instructions on how to email Henry for contributor permissions.

## Helpful Info to Include When Reaching Out

To help resolve your issue faster, please include:

1. **What you were doing** when the issue occurred (e.g., loading screen, mid-run, shop/upgrades, logging in)
2. **Device and browser** (e.g., Chrome on Windows, Safari on iPhone, Chromebook)
3. **Screenshots or error messages**, if available

Thank you for playing and supporting **Flappy Bird Reinvented**!
