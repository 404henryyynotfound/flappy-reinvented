# Project Governance

## Overview

**Flappy Bird Reinvented** is an application-only open-source passion project created and maintained by **Henry Green** (`@404henryyyynotfound`, Xelotics Games). This document explains how decisions are made and how project leadership is structured.

## Current Leadership

* **Lead Maintainer & Creator**: **Henry Green** (`@404henryyyynotfound`)
  * Sets the creative direction, gameplay vision, and development roadmap
  * Reviews and approves contributor applications
  * Reviews, approves, and merges all Pull Requests
  * Manages live deployments, player accounts, Supabase configuration, and leaderboard integrity

* **More Leadership Coming Soon**: Additional co-maintainers and lead contributors will be added over time, depending on who Henry selects from approved project contributors.

## How Decisions Are Made

* **Project Direction & Releases**: All final decisions regarding core gameplay mechanics, branding, releases, and repository access rest with the Lead Maintainer (Henry Green).
* **Contributor Input**: Approved contributors are encouraged to propose ideas, open discussions, and submit Pull Requests. Feedback and suggestions are always welcome, even as final approval stays with project leadership.
* **Community Standards**: Leadership is responsible for upholding our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and keeping the project welcoming, fair, and fun for players and developers alike.

## Becoming a Contributor or Future Leader

1. **Apply to Contribute**: Email **greenhenry441@gmail.com** with your name, GitHub username, and why you want to help (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
2. **Contribute Consistently**: Approved contributors who make helpful, reliable contributions and demonstrate strong teamwork may be invited by Henry to take on future leadership or co-maintainer roles.
